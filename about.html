About Jansarthi AI

Making citizen services easier to understand.

Jansarthi AI is a prototype platform designed to reduce friction between citizens and public services.

The Problem

Citizens may struggle to discover the right service, understand eligibility, identify documents and follow application progress. Information can also be difficult to understand across languages.

Our approach

Jansarthi AI combines a simple service directory, multilingual assistance, guided workflows and request tracking.

AI with guardrails

AI assists — authorised officials decide

Retrieval-based answers

Answers can be grounded in approved service information.

Confidence & fallback

When information is uncertain, the system can direct users to official channels.

Human oversight

Final consequential decisions remain with authorised officials.
